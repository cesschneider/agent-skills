Your coding agent can now generate 4K images and 15-second video clips, and drop them straight into your working folders. No dashboards, no exporting, no renaming files.

The setup getting attention right now combines four pieces: the Higgsfield CLI (generation), a Google Workspace CLI (Sheets/Docs), Claude Code (orchestration), and a tracking spreadsheet that acts as shared memory. Every generation — prompt, model, job ID, cost — gets logged automatically, turning into a searchable creative database over time.

The result: Claude Code stops being a content factory and starts acting like a creative director. It plans the next batch based on what worked last time, adjusts prompts before retrying, and works through a queue of social posts, landing page heroes, and launch videos — checking each result against the brief before moving on.

The bottleneck for small teams was never ideas. It was production time. This collapses a lot of that into one continuous, self-documenting workflow — reportedly moving teams about 75% faster.

My take: don't rebuild your whole stack on day one. Pick one recurring asset — your weekly social graphics, your landing page hero — and run it through an agent with model access for a week. Track it in a sheet, and see how much of that speedup you actually get back.
