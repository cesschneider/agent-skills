## LinkedIn Post

AI agents just had their breakout moment — and most developers haven't caught up yet.

GitHub went from expecting 5% growth in AI-generated code to 3x growth. 17 million AI-generated pull requests in a single month.

I just published a breakdown of a long-form interview with a senior GitHub engineering leader, and one idea stood out above everything else: Spec-Driven Development.

The core analogy: working with AI agents is like 3D printing. You spend most of your time on the CAD drawing — the spec — not on the printing itself. The agent prints. You design.

A good spec has 4 parts:
→ Intent — what are you building, and why?
→ Constraints — what must this never do?
→ Context — what does the agent need to know about your system?
→ Acceptance criteria — how will you know it's correct?

Skip these, and you get vibe-coded software. Fine for a prototype. Not fine for your bank's backend.
